### Genuine application languages

Each language edition uses actual localized Manager, Admin, Customer and Gaming-PC Client captures, with their real application labels and controls. Language selection, persistence and the intended screens were checked in the isolated running applications before capture. No translated overlay stands in for implemented UI.

Synthetic zone, tariff and game-category records were configured for the selected language before capture. This is authored demonstration business content, not automatic translation of arbitrary catalog records. Proper names, PC identifiers, brands and currency codes can remain unchanged. Some dates in the Tajik report context use the browser's Russian formatting fallback; the focused KPI card has no dates. A native Tajik editorial review has not been performed.

The actual product now initializes its document language correctly, uses Inter for Tajik display text and gives long Client action labels full-width rows. The Latin SYBER ARENA wordmark retains Unbounded. These application changes and genuine captures support the selected presentation; they do not establish complete product-wide localization or release readiness.

### Demonstrated operations and limits

The footage comes from real application screens and services in an isolated synthetic environment. PC states and sessions use simulated workstations; no installed Windows Agent, OS lockdown, kiosk behavior or physical-PC enforcement was tested for this film.

The main cut shows a real session start and a real product checkout reaching the Manager bar. **The checkout was captured while connected.** The Cloud-link interruption sequence instead shows a local cash float transaction and its genuine completion feedback, with a working club server and LAN. It is not a customer wallet top-up or evidence that online checkout/payment services work offline. The portrait cut uses a genuine detail from that local cash operation.

The returning connection indicator demonstrates a normal reconnection state. It does not prove lossless synchronization, guaranteed recovery or availability in every failure mode. Online games and services retain their own connectivity requirements. People, balances, activity, products and club records are synthetic; visible totals are not business metrics. The films retain pre-release and synthetic-data disclosures and make no security, fiscal-compliance, adoption or production-availability claim.
