### Picture, language and sound

V3 uses controlled perspective camera moves, layered genuine UI planes, cinematic light paths and animated component connections. It shows the real Manager PC map and session controls, a PC order arriving at the bar, Admin revenue reporting, the Gaming-PC Client and the Customer wallet. The main cut also includes a captured local-desk operation during a Cloud-link interruption and a returning connection indicator; the vertical cut uses an offline Manager map with an explanatory architecture diagram.

The promotional captions, diagram labels and disclosures are localized. **The captured application interfaces remain in English in all three editions.** These are localized advertisements, not a claim that this edit shipped translated application interfaces. There is no voiceover. Tajik captions use a font with the required Cyrillic characters.

The instrumental music and added SFX are original procedural synthesis, with no downloaded music samples, third-party voice recordings or speech. Camera air, panel/glass contacts, interface ticks, session/order confirmations, Cloud-link isolation and reconnection accents follow the picture. The three cuts have their own sound arrangements. Inter and Unbounded are used under the SIL Open Font License.

### Demonstration boundaries

People, balances, branches and games are fictional demonstration data. Contact fields in two desk dialogs were covered before editing. Perspective, lighting, spatial connections and captions are promotional treatment; the underlying interface imagery is genuine. Visible controls establish UI presence, not a live payment-provider or production deployment.

The local-operation sequence requires a working club server and LAN. Online games and services still need their connections. A restored link indicator does not establish lossless synchronization or guaranteed recovery. The films retain pre-release and synthetic-data disclosures and make no security, fiscal-compliance, adoption or scale claim.
