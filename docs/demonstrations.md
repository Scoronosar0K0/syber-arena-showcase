# Demonstration assets

The screenshots in this edition are unmodified frames from genuine local application captures. The videos are described at the end of this page. They are not reconstructed product dashboards.

| File | Interface and content | Context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `manager-pc-map.png` | Staff PC map | Synthetic 24-PC club and fictional player activity |
| `admin-reports.png` | Owner revenue report | Synthetic balances, sales and dates; not customer revenue |
| `pc-client.png` | Player PC interface | Fictional game names and artwork; synthetic session and wallet |
| `customer-wallet.png` | Mobile-sized customer wallet | Synthetic balance and bonus history |

The images were visually reviewed for visible credentials, contact details, private addresses and unrelated third-party game art. They contain only the chosen demonstration views. Menu items and controls indicate UI presence, not a promise that every workflow is production-ready.

These are web encodes of the verified masters, made with the same frames and soundtrack at a lower bitrate for the repository.

**What the films show.** Real application screens captured from an isolated demonstration environment with synthetic data:
- the Manager PC map;
- starting a session at the desk;
- an order placed at a PC arriving at the bar;
- the Admin dashboard;
- the PC Client sign-in;
- the Customer wallet;
- a local cloud-link cut and restore.

People, balances, branches and games are fictional. Synthetic phone numbers that appeared in two desk dialogs were covered before editing. Everything else in those shots is unchanged.

**Original material.** The motion graphics and the soundtrack were made for this project. The soundtrack was synthesized from code, with no samples, loops or third-party audio. The fonts are Unbounded and Inter, used under the SIL Open Font License.

**Wording and limits.** The on-screen text describes demonstrated behaviour only. The cloud-link sequence is one recorded normal case. It does not show that every feature works offline, and it does not guarantee recovery. The films make no claim of production readiness, security, payments, fiscal compliance, adoption or scale. The end card reads "Pre-release product demonstration · Synthetic demo data".

**Checks.** Each delivered file was probed: size, frame rate, duration, codec and audio. Its decoded frames and audio were reviewed:
- every scene and its cuts;
- captions;
- personal-looking data;
- audio levels and the music hits at the cuts.

No human listening session is recorded in these checks.
